AD NUMBER: 2025-02-07
AMENDMENT: 39-23011
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-03-01
SUBJECT: Wing Spar Fitting Inspection
SUPERSEDES: 2024-05-11
[SYNTHETIC SAMPLE - NOT A REAL FAA AD]

(a) Effective Date
This AD is effective March 1, 2025.

(b) Affected ADs
This AD replaces AD 2024-05-11.

(c) Applicability
This AD applies to Airbus SAS Model A320-214 and A320-232 airplanes, certificated in any category, manufacturer serial numbers (MSN) 1000 through 1800 inclusive.

(d) Subject
Air Transport Association (ATA) of America Code 57, Wings.

(e) Unsafe Condition
This AD was prompted by additional reports of cracking in the forward wing spar fitting on airplanes outside the original serial range. We are issuing this AD to detect and correct cracking, which could reduce the structural integrity of the wing.

(f) Compliance
Comply with this AD within the compliance times specified, unless already done.

(g) Inspection
Within 400 flight cycles after the effective date of this AD, do a detailed visual inspection of the forward wing spar fitting for cracking, in accordance with Airbus Service Bulletin A320-57-1301 Revision 02.

(h) Repetitive Inspection
Repeat the inspection required by paragraph (g) of this AD at intervals not to exceed 3,000 flight cycles.

(i) Corrective Action
If any cracking is found during any inspection required by this AD, before further flight, replace the fitting with part number D5727001200100.
