AD NUMBER: 2025-11-03
AMENDMENT: 39-23315
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-07-10
SUBJECT: Engine Fan Blade Inspection
[SYNTHETIC SAMPLE - NOT A REAL FAA AD]

(a) Effective Date
This AD is effective July 10, 2025.

(b) Affected ADs
None.

(c) Applicability
This AD applies to CFM International S.A. Model CFM56-5B4/3 turbofan engines with engine serial numbers 779001 through 779950 inclusive.

(d) Subject
Joint Aircraft System Component (JASC) Code 7230, Turbine Engine Compressor Section.

(e) Unsafe Condition
This AD was prompted by a report of a fan blade liberation. We are issuing this AD to prevent failure of a fan blade, which could result in uncontained debris release and damage to the airplane.

(f) Compliance
Comply with this AD within the compliance times specified, unless already done.

(g) Ultrasonic Inspection
Within 1,500 cycles since last shop visit, perform an ultrasonic inspection of each fan blade dovetail, in accordance with CFM Service Bulletin CFM56-5B S/B 72-1033.
